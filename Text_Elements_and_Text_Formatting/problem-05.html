Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter, its properties, and the changes it undergoes.

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Ammonia: NH3

Atoms and molecules are important concepts in chemistry.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, and interactions.

Area is measured in m2.

Volume is measured in m3.

Scientific notation example: 103 = 1000.

Force, motion, and energy are important topics in physics.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms and their life processes.

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms. Plants and animals are made up of cells.

Photosynthesis is an important process through which green plants prepare their food.
