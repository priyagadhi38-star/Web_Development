Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must maintain proper behaviour inside the college campus.

Students should respect teachers, staff members, and other students.

Attendance

Students must attend classes regularly and maintain the required attendance.

Regular attendance helps students understand lessons and complete their academic work successfully.

Examination

Students must follow all examination instructions and maintain discipline during examinations.

Mobile phones and other unauthorized materials must not be used during examinations.

Assignment Submission

Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.

Assignment deadline: 30 September 2026.

Late submissions may receive a penalty according to the instructor's instructions.

Discipline

Students must maintain discipline and avoid behaviour that disturbs the learning environment.

Students should keep classrooms clean and follow college rules.
Any serious violation may result in appropriate action.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for checking important academic announcements and completing tasks on time.
