New Technology Center Opens in the City

New Center Aims to Support Young Innovators

A new technology center has opened in the city to provide students and young professionals with opportunities to learn about modern technology. The center will conduct educational programs and technical workshops.

The technology center officially opened on 15 August 2026. The event was attended by students, teachers, and technology professionals. The center plans to organize regular learning activities for the community.

The center will focus on programming, digital skills, and innovation. The main goal is to encourage young people to develop practical technology skills. The organizers hope that the center will become an important learning space.

The center is located in Mumbai and will be open throughout the week for scheduled educational activities.
