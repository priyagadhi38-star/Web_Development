Breaking News: College Launches New Technology Innovation Centre

New Centre to Support Student Projects and Innovation

ABC College has officially announced the launch of a new Technology Innovation Centre to provide students with opportunities to develop innovative projects and improve their technical skills.

The new centre is located on the college campus and will be available to students from different departments. The facility provides a space where students can work on academic projects, technology experiments and creative ideas.

The centre was officially opened on 31 August 2026 at the ABC College campus. Students and faculty members attended the opening event
