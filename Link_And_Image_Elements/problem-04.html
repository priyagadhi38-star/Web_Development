Agra Travel Guide

Agra is a famous destination in India known for its historical monuments, rich culture and traditional food. It is an excellent destination for a weekend trip.

Attractions

The Taj Mahal is one of Agra's most famous attractions. Visitors can also explore Agra Fort and other historical places around the city.

Culture

Agra has a rich historical and cultural background. The city reflects Mughal architecture and traditions through its monuments, markets and local crafts.

Food

Visitors can enjoy traditional North Indian dishes and local sweets. Agra is also known for its famous sweet called petha.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is the recommended season for visiting Agra because the weather is generally more comfortable for sightseeing.

Travel Tips

Travel Tip: Start sightseeing early in the day to comfortably visit major attractions and avoid the busiest periods.

Carry drinking water, wear comfortable clothing and keep enough time for visiting the historical monuments.
