Registration Process

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Create your participant account.
  3. Select the competition.
  4. Enter your participant details.
  5. Pay the registration fee, if applicable.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.

Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry their valid college identity card.
  • Participants must report at the venue before the scheduled time.
  • Maintain discipline throughout the event.
  • Follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Damage to college property is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must follow event-specific rules.

Event Definitions

Open Event
An event open to all eligible participants.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a team.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially entering a participant into an event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing a particular event.
Final Round
The final stage in which selected participants compete for the winning position.
