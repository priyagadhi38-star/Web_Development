Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Bring the required notebook.
  3. Check the assigned computer.
  4. Complete the given experiment.
  5. Save your work and submit it.

Classroom Rules

  • Attendance is mandatory.
  • Maintain discipline inside the classroom.
  • Do not use mobile phones during lectures.
  • Keep the classroom clean.
  • Submit assignments on time.

Important Notice: Internal Assessment submissions must be completed before the announced deadline.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session focused mainly on theory.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments.
Tutorial
A smaller learning session for practice and discussion.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the academic semester.
Practical
A session where students apply theoretical knowledge practically.
