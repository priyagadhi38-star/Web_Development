Admission Overview

ABC University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various fields. Students should carefully check eligibility requirements and complete the application before the deadline.

"Choose your course carefully and complete every admission requirement on time." — Admission Office

Eligibility

Mandatory: Applicants must have completed the required qualifying examination for their selected program.

  • Minimum required academic qualification
  • Valid identification document
  • Required entrance examination score, if applicable
  • Other course-specific requirements

Application Deadline: 30 September 2026

Application Process

  1. Visit the official admission website.
  2. Register for a new account.
  3. Fill in the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Download and keep the application receipt.

Required Documents

  • Class 10 certificate
  • Class 12 certificate
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Valid identity proof
  • Transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions a student must satisfy to apply for a course.
Merit List
A list of candidates selected according to their qualifying performance.
Counselling
The process through which eligible students select courses or seats.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for admission.
